# Company policy

This sample document contains fictional policies for the company North.

## Leave

### Request deadlines

Employees must request leave at least 14 calendar days before it starts. Employees on probation must request leave at least 21 calendar days in advance. These deadlines apply to planned leave.

### Approval

The direct manager approves leave. Once approved, the employee submits the request to HR. Until approval is received, travel bookings should allow cancellation.

## Business trips

### Trip approval

The department manager approves business trips. The employee submits a travel request 7 working days before departure, including the purpose, dates, and estimated expenses.

### Expense reimbursement

Receipts and tickets are required for reimbursement of business trip expenses. The expense report must be submitted to accounting within 3 working days of returning.

## Work equipment

To receive a work laptop, the employee opens a request with the IT department. IT responds within 2 working days. When reporting a broken laptop, include its serial number and a description of the problem.

## Training

The company reimburses approved courses up to 300 euros per year. Written approval from the manager is required before paying for a course. Courses paid for without prior approval are not reimbursed.
